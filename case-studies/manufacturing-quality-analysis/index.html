Manufacturing Quality & Defect Trend Analysis

A demonstration of structured quality data analysis using synthetic assembly line defect counts, Pareto frequency prioritization (80/20 rule), and Fishbone root-cause exploration.

Synthetic Data DemonstrationPareto PrioritizationDefect CategorizationRoot-Cause Framework
Confidentiality & Ethics NoticeAll data, line identifiers, and defect classifications in this case study are 100% synthetic and created purely for portfolio demonstration. They do not represent Panasonic Industrial Devices Batam or any real commercial facility.

Demonstration Objective

This self-initiated project demonstrates how manufacturing domain knowledge is applied to organize raw inspection logs, identify defect concentrations using the Pareto principle (80/20 rule), and structure collaborative root-cause investigations.

Root Cause Analysis Framework (Ishikawa Fishbone)

Focusing engineering efforts on the #1 issue—Solder Bridging (43% of defects)—a multi-branch Ishikawa framework organizes hypotheses across the classic 4M manufacturing categories:

Machine Factors
  • Squeegee blade wear resulting in uneven solder paste print pressure.
  • Stencil separation speed too high, causing paste drag across fine pads.
  • Conveyor vibration during transfer into reflow oven zone.
Material Factors
  • Solder paste viscosity degradation due to extended exposure on stencil.
  • PCB pad oxidation or solder mask thickness variance between board lots.
  • Flux activity decay in high-temperature ambient conditions.
Method Factors
  • Inadequate stencil under-wipe cleaning frequency (wiping every 10 boards instead of 5).
  • Suboptimal reflow thermal profile with excessive ramp rate causing paste slumping.
  • Misaligned fiducial offset calibration during setup changeover.
Man (Operator & Process)
  • Shift handover inconsistency in recording solder paste thaw timing.
  • Manual visual inspection fatigue during end-of-shift cycles.
  • Training variance on stencil cleaning verification standards.

Corrective Action Recommendation Framework

By pairing Pareto prioritization with root-cause categorization, plant teams avoid scattering resources across low-impact issues. Instead, an actionable three-step remediation plan is developed:

  1. Immediate Containment: Increase automatic stencil wipe frequency from 10 to 5 boards and enforce digital thaw-time logging.
  2. Preventive Engineering: Conduct reflow oven thermal profiling to optimize preheat soak temperature and prevent paste slumping.
  3. Standardization: Update digital work instructions and train operators on visual squeegee inspection criteria.
